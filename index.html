About

An HRM and Business Administration professional whose path into teaching has been built deliberately. Academic grounding through BBA and MBA studies is paired with active research on AI in HR, sustainable HRM, and gender and career advancement, alongside industry exposure and a long-standing commitment to mentoring and guiding others.

Teaching Philosophy

I believe teaching works best when it stays honest about the gap between theory and practice, and helps students close it themselves. My own research trains me to ask questions before offering answers, and I want my classroom to work the same way: grounded in real organizational problems, open to debate, and generous with mentorship outside class hours. I see teaching and research as one activity, not two, and I want students to leave my courses more curious than when they arrived.
